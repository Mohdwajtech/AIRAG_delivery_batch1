# Leave Policy

Employees get 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Annual leave accrues
monthly and is prorated by start date. Unused annual leave does not roll over
past December 31 of each year.

In addition to annual leave, employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per
year. Sick leave does not carry over. Requests for leave should be submitted at
least two weeks in advance where possible and require manager approval.
