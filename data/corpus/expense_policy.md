# Expense Policy

Employees may be reimbursed for reasonable business expenses. Any expense over
$50 requires an itemized receipt to be reimbursed. Expenses under $50 may be
claimed without a receipt but must still be logged.

Reimbursement requests are submitted through the expense portal and require
manager approval. Travel, client meals, and software subscriptions are eligible;
personal items are not. Approved reimbursements are paid within 30 days.
