# BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) Policy

Employees may use personal devices for work under the BYOD policy, provided the
device is enrolled in the company's Mobile Device Management (MDM) system and has
full-disk encryption enabled.

Personal devices must have a screen lock, run a supported operating system, and
receive security updates. The company can remotely wipe work data from an
enrolled device if it is lost, stolen, or when the employee leaves.
